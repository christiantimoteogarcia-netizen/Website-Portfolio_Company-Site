About Transformer

Each WAGNER Transformer is a product perfected through years of experience and technical innovation in the field of transformer manufacturing. Incorporating the latest development in electrical design and materials, WAGNER Transformers meet the increasingly stringent demands of modern electrical distribution systems.

Our Transformer

Transformers

Our distribution transformers are engineered for high efficiency, long-term reliability, and optimal performance in industrial, commercial, and residential applications. Built with premium materials and tested under rigorous standards, each unit ensures safe and stable voltage transformation.

Our Story

Since 1998

A Plant Office mainly distributing small powered Transformers up to 2kVA. Since the establishment in August 1998, Super Manufacturing INC. has significantly grown, providing superior products and services and have achieved significant milestones.

Our Commitment

Super Manufacturing Incorporated is committed to continuously upgrade and improve our product and services, dedicated to produce defect-free and highly efficient transformers at the most competitive cost possible, in order to meet and even to exceed customer expectation.
